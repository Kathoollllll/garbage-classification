🌿 Our Mission

About GreenGuard

Using artificial intelligence to make responsible waste disposal accessible to everyone.

Why We Built This

Improper waste disposal is one of the leading contributors to environmental pollution. Most people want to recycle correctly but simply don't know which bin to use or how to handle hazardous materials.

GreenGuard bridges that gap. By combining computer vision and deep learning, we make it effortless to identify waste and get the right disposal guidance — instantly, from any device.

🌍♻️🌱💚

The Model

Built with TensorFlow and trained on real-world waste images

🧠

Architecture

Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) fine-tuned for multi-class image classification across 7 waste categories.

🖼️

Input

Images are resized to 150×150 pixels and normalized before being passed to the model for inference.

📊

Output

The model returns a predicted category and a confidence score, giving you a clear picture of the result.

🗂️

Training Data

Trained on a curated dataset of labeled waste images spanning Battery, Cardboard, Clothes, Glass, Metal, Paper, and Plastic.

Supported Categories

Seven waste types, each with tailored disposal guidance

🔋

Battery

Hazardous — take to designated collection points. Never bin with regular trash.

📦

Cardboard

Flatten boxes and keep dry. Goes in the blue recycling bin.

👕

Clothes

Donate wearable items. Worn-out textiles go to fabric recycling points.

🍾

Glass

Rinse containers and separate by color if required locally. Green bin.

🥫

Metal

Rinse cans and crush them. Aluminum and steel are infinitely recyclable.

📄

Paper

Keep clean and dry. Avoid wax-coated or laminated paper in recycling.

🧴

Plastic

Rinse containers and check the recycling number on the bottom.
